# Introduce what you can do with Egbe

Use this as a quality example, not a fixed script. Speak in the founder's language
and in the first person. Connect **Egbe's infrastructure, tools and workflows**
to **results you can deliver**; generic model abilities or skill IDs do not explain
its value. Use a compact table grouped by category, as below; adapt its rows
and examples to current public discovery and the founder's context.

Distinguish platform capability from access in this chat and integrations still
needed in the product. Before verification, do not imply everything is configured,
available to this account or free. Adapt the closing to actual setup progress;
do not announce installation in an environment that cannot perform it.

## Example

With Egbe, I can help you go **from an idea to a working product and your first
customers** — or grow a product you've already launched.

Egbe gives me more than instructions: it provides ready-to-use infrastructure
for your product, research and marketing tools, and workflows that connect them.
Here's what you can ask me to do:

| Area | What Egbe provides | What I can do for you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idea & market | Search, data collection and market research workflows | Check demand, study competitors, and propose an audience and offer for your first test. |
| Product & design | Planning workflows and a library of design references | Turn an idea into a product plan and design the interface. |
| Build & launch | Hosting, code repositories, databases and a website address | Build and publish a website or app, then ship improvements. |
| AI features | Access to AI models and image generation | Add an assistant, document analysis or content generation to your product. |
| Sign-in & payments | Google sign-in and Stripe integrations | Set up user login, payments and subscriptions. |
| First customers | Advertising integrations, B2B prospect search and email campaigns | Find potential customers, prepare and launch approved ads or outreach, and evaluate the results. |
| Growth & monitoring | Analytics, error tracking and regular reports | Find where signups and purchases drop off, propose improvements, and set up updates on your product's health. |

### Closing when full setup is available

I'll check what's available for your project and in this environment, then
continue setup. After that, I'll suggest a useful first step for your business.

### Closing when a project workspace is needed

Here, I can introduce you to what Egbe offers. To start working on your product,
open Codex, choose a project folder — an empty one is fine — and send it the
