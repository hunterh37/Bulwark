Selection was by measurement (bass energy jump, tempo). I cannot hear audio, so none of these were judged by ear.

Research result: the web search found no source naming EDM or dubstep as the launch-video norm. One commissioned tech-launch track was described as electronic with suspense, build and impact. Two 2026 reviews of launch videos say heavy dramatic music reads as performance.

Sources reachable from this machine without an account are Mixkit, Wikimedia Commons and archive.org. Pixabay returns 403. NCS tracks need a paid license for brand use.

Two routes to a stronger track:
1. `npx hyperframes auth login` (free). That opens the HeyGen catalog of 10k+ tracks and prompt-generated music, e.g. "dark dubstep, 140 bpm, 8-bar riser, hard drop".
2. Put any licensed track at `promo/source/music/`. I detect its drop and tempo, move the cuts onto its beat grid, and land the drop on the product reveal in both cuts.

After a pick, the film is regridded to the track tempo: montage cuts on beats, a short gap before the drop, drop on the portal reveal, second hit on the end card.
