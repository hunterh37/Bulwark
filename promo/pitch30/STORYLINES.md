# Storylines for the 30 s pitch cut

Built: A. B and C are words only.

## A. The grid at dusk (built)

Type carries the argument, cut on the beat. Colour photographs of real sites stand for the four sectors on the landing page; the viewer sees what the code controls, then the product checking it.

- First two seconds: pylons against a setting sun, "SOFTWARE" on frame 0, "RUNS", "THE GRID" on the next two beats.
- Beats: hook, four sectors with their protocols, question, drop to the name, scan, findings, Edge, price, end card.
- Look: full-bleed dusk and night photography, white Inter 900, volt on protocol names and the brand panels.
- Sound: house at 120 bpm, breakdown for 8 s, drop on the name, riser into the drop, clicks and pops on UI actions.

## B. One finding, start to fix

A moment in someone's day, as one continuous take. A commit lands with `security_mode = None`, the camera follows it into the portal, the finding appears with file and line, the fix text scrolls up.

- First two seconds: a diff line turning red inside an editor.
- Beats: commit, scan, finding, remediation text, re-scan clean, end card.
- Look: product UI only on ink, one camera, no photographs.
- Sound: minimal house, bass enters on the finding.

## C. $0

A number lands. The film opens on the price and works backward to what it buys.

- First two seconds: "$0" filling the frame on volt.
- Beats: price, three repos, the modules by name, findings table, Edge, end card.
- Look: volt and ink colour fields, no photographs, type at 40% of frame height.
- Sound: house with a filtered intro, full kit on the modules.
