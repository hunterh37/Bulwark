01 / Mark

The wall and the bolt

A battlement for defence, a bolt cut through it for the grid. The mark is always a solid fill; never outlined, rotated or recolored outside the four lockups below.

05 / Icons

Icons and grid motif

24 px grid, 1.75 px square-cap stroke, no fills, no rounded corners. Backgrounds carry a half-inch schematic grid at low contrast, like a single-line diagram.
